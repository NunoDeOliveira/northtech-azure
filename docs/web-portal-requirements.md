## Web portal requirements asociated


**Functional Requirements**

RF01 - The portal must display NorthTech's services to all visitors without authentication.
RF02 - Visitors must be able to submit enquiries through a public contact form.
RF03 - Customers must log in securely to access their private area.
RF04 - Customers must be able to view their contracted services.
RF05 - The application must store customer information, contracted services, and enquiries.

**No Functional Requirements**

RNF01 - All data in transit must be encrypted.
RNF02 - The portal must support multiple concurrent users.
RNF03 - The infrastructure must automatically scale based on demand.
