## Web portal requirements asociated

**Functional Requirements**

RF01 - Visitors must be able to view the service catalog without authentication.
RF02 - Visitors must be able to send enquiries through a contact form.
RF03 - Customers must be able to log in securely.
RF04 - Customers must be able to view their contracted services.
RF05 - The system must store customer information, contracts, and enquiries.
RF06 - Customers must only be able to access their own contracts.

**Non-Functional Requirements**

RNF01 - All data in transit must be encrypted.
RNF02 - The portal must support multiple concurrent users.
RNF03 - The infrastructure must scale automatically based on demand.
RNF04 - The portal must support updates with minimal downtime.
RNF05 - The portal must provide logs and metrics for monitoring.
RNF06 - The portal must work on desktop and mobile devices.
